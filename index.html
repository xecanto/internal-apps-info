Internal Reporting Tools

This page belongs to a set of private, internal tools used by a single business to run its own operations: profit-and-loss reporting, inventory reconciliation and related workflow automations.

The tools are built on Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Google BigQuery. They are used only by the business's own team members, who are given access individually. They are not a public service, and there is nothing to sign up for here.

When a team member signs in, Google shows a consent screen listing the permissions the tools use. How data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
