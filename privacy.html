Privacy Policy

Internal Reporting Tools · Last updated 22 October 2026

Who uses these tools

These are private tools used internally by one business and its authorised team members. They are not offered to the public.

What the tools access

With the permissions a team member grants on Google's consent screen, the tools:

  • read and write the business's own Google Sheets and Google Drive files used for reporting;
  • read and write the business's own Google BigQuery datasets;
  • connect to external services the business uses — such as its Amazon seller account — to retrieve its own sales, inventory and settlement reports.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce the business's internal reports and to run its own operational workflows. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with any third party except the service providers the tools run on (Google) or retrieve the business's own data from (such as Amazon).

Where the data is stored

Data stays in the business's own Google Cloud project, Google Sheets and Google Drive, protected by Google account permissions. Only team members the business has granted access can read it.

Google user data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A team member can revoke the tools' access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
